## User story

As an owner, I want to switch between salons I own, so that each shop has its own profile, queue, and QR without mixing them.

## Acceptance criteria

- An owner who owns more than one salon can select which salon is in context.
- Hours, workers, services, pending queue, and QR for the selected salon do not mix with another salon they own.
- Each salon stays a separate customer-facing profile (not a chain “one brand, many locations” with shared workers).
- An owner with a single salon does not need a switcher to use owner home.

## Out of scope

- Chain multi-location / shared workers across sites (Phase 2)
- Receptionist or manager roles (Phase 2)
- Public “Register salon”
- Customer-facing salon picker for owners
