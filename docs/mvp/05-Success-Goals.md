# Success Goals & Metrics

## Primary Leading Indicator

**"Salon profile viewed → request sent."** Not app downloads. With a small, free early base, this conversion signal tells us whether the discovery-and-request flow is actually working, before there's enough volume for lagging metrics (repeat bookings, retention) to mean much.

## Cold-Start / Supply-Side Goals

- Founder-led onboarding of the **first 15–20 Sarajevo salons**, regardless of neighborhood clustering (the "near you" discovery mechanic was chosen specifically so a scattered early salon list still feels locally useful to each user).
- Each onboarding visit doubles as organic marketing content (reel/photo/story), building a sense of momentum before there's real scale.

## Owner-Side Health Signals

- **Response speed**: how quickly an owner accepts or counter-proposes after a request comes in (feeds the future "Fast Responder" badge and is already known to correlate with IG DM conversion).
- **QR scan → verified-visit conversion rate**: a concrete, owner-visible read on whether their counter sticker is actually driving reconnects.
- **Cancellation / no-show rate** and **decline rate** over time, once there's enough volume to be meaningful.

## Customer-Side Health Signals

- Request → confirmed-booking conversion (i.e., how often a request ends confirmed — via accept or counter-propose acceptance — not rejected or ignored).
- Repeat-salon rate (feeds the "Regular" badge logic and Favorites usage).
- Verification completion rate (phone + email) — this is a new friction point (see `08-Improvements-and-Open-Questions.md`) and should be watched closely once live, since it sits right before the request is sent.

## What We're Explicitly NOT Optimizing For at MVP

- Revenue / monetization — pricing is free at launch by design; there is no revenue target yet.
- App Store presence / native install numbers — not applicable, PWA only.
- Broad geographic reach — success is scoped to Sarajevo density first, BiH second.

## Open Question — Not Yet Defined

- No concrete numeric targets (e.g. "X% profile-view-to-request conversion by week Y") have been set yet. This is reasonable to leave open until the 90-day phased marketing plan is built (see `MarketingBrainstorm.md`), but should be revisited once that plan exists — flagging here rather than inventing numbers now.
